# Three new exhibits: a note for the founder

**Status:** proposal for review. Nothing has been published to Sanity.
**Companion file:** `docs/content/new-exhibits.json` (the full text, choices, tags, links and plate briefs).

## The three exhibits

**The Toaster That Prints Notes From Your Future Self** (Civic Time wing)
A municipal toaster that browns a short message onto your toast, written by you a year from now. You either read the note while it is warm or butter over it without reading.

**The Switchboard for Conversations That Ended Too Soon** (Counterfactual Communications wing)
An old operator's switchboard that reconnects conversations at the exact word where they stopped. This is the first exhibit with three choices: answer the one line still lit, pull every cord at once, or try the one jack with no label.

**The Kettle That Brews the Weather of Past Visits** (Domestic Weather Memory wing)
An enamel kettle that remembers the weather outside each time a guest was offered tea, and lets it back into the room when it boils. You either put the kettle on or leave it cold on its trivet.

## What changed in the old walk

Three old endings now point to a new exhibit instead of an old one. Their text and tags are untouched; only the "Continue to" door moves.

1. "A missed call arrives from you" (Telephone) now sends you to the **Toaster**: a call from your future self leads to notes from your future self.
2. "The machine keeps humming" (Vending Machine) now sends you to the **Kettle**: a weekend you kept is a weekend for visitors.
3. "The room rains back" (Umbrella) now sends you to the **Switchboard**: a voice calling you inside never finished its sentence.

Each of the three old exhibits still has one door leading to another old exhibit, so the old triangle (Vending Machine → Telephone → Umbrella → Vending Machine) still works as a loop. From any of the six exhibits you can reach every other one by following the doors. No ending sends you back to the exhibit you are standing at.

Each exhibit is now the destination of two endings, except the Switchboard, which is the destination of three.

## New ticket phrases

Eight new tags, each with a phrase for the ticket in the same style as the old ones:

- `advised-in-advance`: "advised in advance by someone who writes like you"
- `thursday-rearranged`: "with one Thursday already rearranged"
- `unadvised`: "unadvised by anyone, including yourself"
- `sentence-finished`: "with an old sentence finally allowed to end"
- `left-unfinished-on-purpose`: "leaving several things unfinished on purpose"
- `conversation-pending`: "with a conversation waiting for you further on"
- `counted-as-a-guest`: "counted among the guests who stayed for a second cup"
- `visits-without-weather`: "visited by friends who leave no weather behind"

Five old tags come back in the new endings, so tickets from the old and new rooms connect: `quiet-refusal` (butter it unread, leave the kettle cold), `apology-not-required`, `room-to-breathe`, `line-left-open`, `shelter-returned`.
